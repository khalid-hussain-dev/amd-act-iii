# Synthetic operating procedures for the LotLens demo

> These short procedures are fictional demonstration material. They are not approved factory procedures and must not be used to operate real equipment.

## QSP-17 §4.2 — Quality trend review

When a measured defect rate remains above its established baseline or process control limit across three consecutive inspection samples, notify the quality lead and identify the affected lot and time window. Compare inspection records with the previous stable interval before concluding that a cause has been established. Record the measurements used in the review.

## QSP-17 §4.3 — Containment decision

The quality lead reviews the affected lot, inspection evidence, and current operating conditions before deciding whether additional inspection or temporary containment is appropriate. Do not treat an automated alert as authorization to stop production, release product, or place a hold.

## OPS-04 §2.6 — Sensor reading review

If temperature, pressure, or vibration readings change near a quality alert, compare the readings with the equipment's documented normal range and recent maintenance record. A coincident change may help guide an investigation but does not prove a root cause. Escalate readings outside approved equipment limits to the responsible technician.

## QSP-17 §5.1 — Shift handoff

A shift handoff should state the affected line and lot, the time window reviewed, the measured evidence, any source procedure consulted, unresolved questions, and the next human review owner. Clearly distinguish observed facts from hypotheses and proposed checks.
